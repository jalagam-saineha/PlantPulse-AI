Quick start
  1. Add a plant — name, species and a photo.
  2. Run a Leaf Analysis — upload a clear, close photo of a leaf against a plain background.
  3. Review the Visual Health Score, metrics, and Health Map on the results page.
  4. Optionally start a Growth Scan with a short time-lapse video.
  5. Track changes in Health Timeline and Compare Analyses.
  6. Generate a PDF from any analysis result page, and find it later in Reports.
About the measurements

Leaf Coverage, Yellowing, Visible Damage and Dark Spots are computed directly from the uploaded image using computer vision (OpenCV color segmentation) — they are real measurements of that specific photo, not estimates or lookups.

The Visual Health Score is a transparent weighted combination of those measurements (see "How is this calculated?" on any result page). It is a visual screening tool, not a certified agricultural or scientific diagnosis.

Gemini Vision analyzes uploaded plant images when the provider is configured. Its visual estimates are uncertain screening guidance, not a confirmed diagnosis.

Tips for better results
  • Fill the frame with the leaf; avoid busy backgrounds.
  • Use even, natural lighting — avoid harsh shadows or direct glare.
  • For Growth Studio, keep the camera angle and distance consistent between recordings.
  • Re-analyze the same plant every few days for the most useful trend insights.
